About Me

Jiajun Xu, Florian
Master of Human Computer Interaction
University of Technology Sydney

My research focuses on web agents, generative web systems, video understanding, and multimodal evaluation, with an emphasis on understanding and diagnosing the reliability and failure modes of generative systems in interactive environments.

I also have a long-standing interest in creative coding, computational music, and generative audiovisual systems.🎶

I am working on:

Multimodal Evaluation
  • Fine-grained benchmarks for identifying failures in generated content.
Video-Grounded Diagnosis
  • Diagnosing generated interactive websites using video evidence and source-code verification. [VideoVIBE]
Audio-Video Generation
  • Fine-grained evaluation of multi-talker dialogues. [MTAVG-Bench]
  • Diagnosing failures of cinematic expressiveness. [MTAVG-Bench 2.0]
